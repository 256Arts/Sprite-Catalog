# Sprite Catalog

<img src="https://www.256arts.com/spritecatalog/icon_scatalog.png" alt="Sprite Catalog icon" width="128" align="right">

A browsable catalog of pixel-art sprites and pixel fonts, ready to drag into your game or export as iMessage stickers.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/sprite-catalog/id1560692872) · [256arts.com/spritecatalog](https://www.256arts.com/spritecatalog/)

## Features

- **Curated sprite catalog** — browse hundreds of pixel-art sprites organized into collections like Food, Tiles, Artwork, and Treasure.
- **Pixel fonts** — a library of pixel fonts shown as full alphabets, not just a thumbnail.
- **Import your own sprites** — add your own pixel art to the catalog, synced across your devices with iCloud.
- **Spritesheet cutter** — slice a spritesheet image into individual sprites by pixel size and spacing.
- **iMessage stickers** — send catalog and imported sprites as stickers right from Messages.
- **Palettes** — browse a library of premade color palettes.
- **Drag and drop** — drag any sprite out of the app as a PNG, or save it to My Collection.
- **Shortcuts & Siri** — cut a spritesheet into sprites from the Shortcuts app.
- iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. iMessage stickers are available on iPhone and iPad.

## Building

Open `Sprite Catalog.xcodeproj` in the latest Xcode (beta) and run the `Sprite Catalog` scheme. It depends on one remote Swift package, **PaletteKit**. See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) for the architecture.
